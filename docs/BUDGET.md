# Snowball: game budget

Development funding request. Estimate by Design Master Solutions LLC for taking the jam prototype to a released game in 6 months.
Blended studio rate: US$ 45/hour. All amounts in US dollars.

| # | Work package | Hours | Cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Game design and balance: more life events (rent, first job, children, raise), difficulty curve, 3 life paths | 120 | 5,400 |
| 2 | Original art: pixel-art skier, slope themes (desert dunes, city, mountain), UI, store art | 200 | 9,000 |
| 3 | Original music and sound effects, mixed for phones | 80 | 3,600 |
| 4 | Arabic and English: review of the jam's Arabic edition by a native speaker and an Islamic-finance adviser, the new content in both languages, right-to-left UI for it | 90 | 4,050 |
| 5 | Mobile release: iOS and Android builds, store listings, accessibility (color-blind safe, one-hand play) | 160 | 7,200 |
| 6 | Classroom mode: teacher dashboard with no personal data, 10-minute lesson plan, printable debrief | 140 | 6,300 |
| 7 | Playtests: 3 rounds with learners 16-30, before/after questions on the 4 lessons, fixes | 100 | 4,500 |
| 8 | QA, performance on low-end phones, launch and 3 months of support | 110 | 4,950 |
|   | **Subtotal** | **1,000** | **45,000** |
|   | Contingency (10%) | | 4,500 |
|   | **Total** | | **49,500** |

## Milestones

- Month 1: design and balance, Arabic/English text, first playtest on the web build.
- Month 2-3: art and sound, classroom mode.
- Month 4: mobile builds, second playtest.
- Month 5: QA, accessibility, third playtest.
- Month 6: store release and the playtest report (what learners got right before and after playing).

## What ships at each funding level

Prizes and final amounts are set by the jam, so the plan is cut from the bottom of its priority list, not spread thin.
Learning and reach come first; new content comes last.

| Funding | Lines funded | What ships |
|-----------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| $5,000 | Line 4 ($4,050) + 21 hours of line 7 ($945) = $4,995 | The Arabic edition reviewed, one small playtest on the web build |
| $15,000 | Lines 4, 6 and 7 = $14,850 | Plus classroom mode and all three playtest rounds, on the web |
| $30,000 | Lines 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 = $27,000 + 10% contingency = $29,700 | Plus the iOS and Android release with the current art |
| $49,500 | All lines + 10% contingency | Plus new life events, original art and original music |

## What stays free

The web version stays free to play, with no ads and no data collection from players.
